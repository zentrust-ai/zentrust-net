SMS Communications

SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: April 13, 2026

What messages will I receive?

Zentrust sends text messages on behalf of wedding venues and catering companies that use our platform. Messages are strictly limited to:

  • Booking confirmations when you schedule a tour or tasting
  • Appointment reminders the day before your scheduled visit
  • Invoice and payment notifications related to your event
  • Form completion reminders (e.g., tasting preference forms)

We do not send marketing, promotional, or advertising messages via SMS. All text messages are transactional and directly related to your booking activity.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your booking activity. You will receive no more than 6 messages per booking. A typical booking generates 2-3 messages (confirmation + reminder).

How you opt in

You will only receive text messages if you explicitly opt in during the booking process. Here is how consent is collected:

1
Complete a booking You schedule a tour or tasting through the AI concierge chat widget on a venue's website.
2
SMS opt-in prompt appears After your booking is confirmed, the chat widget presents a dedicated SMS opt-in card asking: "Would you like to receive text message reminders about your upcoming visit?"
3
You choose Yes or No The opt-in card displays clear Yes and No buttons. Only users who tap "Yes" are enrolled for text messages. The card includes the disclosure: "Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time."
4
Consent is recorded Your opt-in choice is stored in our database with a timestamp and your session ID. You can opt out at any time.
The SMS opt-in card is embedded within our AI concierge chat widget, which is live on venue websites including grandmanorevents.com. The widget is accessible to all website visitors without requiring login or account creation.

How to opt out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time using any of these methods:

  • Reply STOP to any text message you receive from us
  • Reply any of these keywords: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REVOKE
  • Click the unsubscribe link in any email communication from us

After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message: "You have successfully been unsubscribed. You will not receive any more messages from this number. Reply START to resubscribe."

How to get help

Reply HELP or INFO to any text message for assistance. You can also contact us directly:

Zentrust LLC
Email: support@zentrust.net
Web: zentrust.net

Rates & charges

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Zentrust does not charge any fees for receiving text messages. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Supported carriers include, but are not limited to: AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, and all major US carriers.

Privacy

Your phone number and opt-in status are stored securely and are never sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than sending the transactional messages described above. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
